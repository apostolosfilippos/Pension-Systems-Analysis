Pension Systems Analysis: Sustainability & Adequacy

📊 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes the relationship between Public Pension Spending and the Net Replacement Rate across 39 countries. The goal is to visualize how different pension architectures (PAYG, Mixed, Funded) affect national budgets and retiree income.

🔍 Key Insights

Fiscal Pressure: Funded systems are significantly more cost-effective for the state, with public spending averaging ~4% of GDP, whereas PAYG systems often exceed 13-15%. Replacement Rates: High replacement rates (e.g., Greece, Netherlands) can be achieved through different models, but the fiscal burden varies dramatically. Sustainability: Countries utilizing Mixed or Funded models demonstrate better long-term budget resilience in the face of aging demographics.

🛠 System Definitions

PAYG (Pay-As-You-Go): Current workers’ taxes fund today’s pensions. Funded: Individual accounts where personal savings are invested for the future. Mixed: A hybrid model combining a basic state pension with private or occupational savings.

📂 Data Source

Source: OECD (2024), Pensions at a Glance. Metrics: Public Pension Spending (% of GDP) vs. Net Replacement Rate (%) for average earners.

🚀 Visuals Included

Scatter Plot: Correlation between spending and pension adequacy. Summary Table: Average metrics grouped by system type. Category Analysis: Comparative view of fiscal sustainability.
