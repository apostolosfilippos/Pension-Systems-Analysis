Global Pension Systems - Data Insights & Sustainability

📊 Project Overview

I built a Power BI dashboard to compare how different countries fund their pension systems.
The goal was to measure Efficiency: How much does the government spend (Public Spending) versus what the citizen actually gets back (Replacement Rate).

🔍 Key Ιnsights

  System Efficiency: I used data to show that Funded models (private/individual accounts) achieve high retirement income with only ~4% GDP cost, whereas PAYG models (state-run) often require over 15% GDP to deliver similar results.
  Transition Value: My analysis quantifies the impact of shifting to Defined Contribution (DC) models. Data shows this transition can lead to a 17% reduction in government pension liabilities while securing long-term solvency for aging populations.
  Risk Mapping: I categorized 39 countries into performance tiers, highlighting Greece and Italy as high-expenditure outliers compared to more sustainable "Mixed" models like the Netherlands.

🛠 Skills & Tools

  Dashboard Design: Created interactive scatter plots to identify correlations between spending and benefits.
  Data Synthesis: Merged complex OECD financial metrics into a single, easy-to-read heatmap for decision-makers.
  Business Logic: Applied "Life Expectancy" and "Aging Demographics" variables to explain the necessity of pension reforms.
