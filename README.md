# Global Pension Systems - Data Insights & Sustainability

## 📊 Project Overview
I developed a Power BI dashboard to evaluate the fiscal efficiency of global pension structures. The analysis focuses on the trade-off between **Public Spending (% of GDP)** and **Net Income Replacement Rates**, providing a clear view of how different funding models impact state budgets and retiree adequacy.

## 🔍 Key Insights
* **System Efficiency:** Data reveals that **Funded models** (private/individual accounts) achieve stable retirement income with a low public cost of ~4.2% GDP, whereas **PAYG/NDC models** often exceed 10-12% GDP to deliver similar adequacy levels.
* **Transition Value:** The analysis quantifies the impact of shifting toward Defined Contribution (DC) and Mixed models, showcasing a path to reduce government liabilities while securing long-term solvency for aging populations.
* **Risk Mapping:** Categorized 39 countries into **performance quadrants**, highlighting Greece and Italy as high-expenditure outliers compared to highly efficient "Mixed" models like the Netherlands.

## 🛠 Skills & Tools
* **Dashboard Design:** Developed interactive quadrant charts to identify correlations between fiscal spending and benefit adequacy.
* **Data Synthesis:** Merged complex OECD financial metrics into a single, executive-ready visualization for strategic decision-making.
* **Business Logic:** Applied demographic variables to explain the necessity of structural pension reforms in aging economies.
